(05)About the project6 questions

Questions
& answers

01How are JOYCITY and JC SPACE connected?

JOYCITY invests every ruble it can spare in JC SPACE. Thank you to JOYCITY customers: your support keeps us moving forward.

02What do a marketplace brand and China logistics have to do with space?

JOYCITY directs every ruble it can spare, plus part of the profit from every pair of shoes sold, to JC SPACE. Both ventures were founded by Arsen Sarukhanyan, an entrepreneur with a background in consumer goods, logistics and business with China.

03What is the goal, and how will it help the country?

The goal is a space launch system with an ultra-light launch vehicle and a recoverable first stage to deliver spacecraft to orbit. We will report results as each development stage is completed.

04Where does the funding for private space come from?

Our own funds, with no external investment. JOYCITY directs every ruble it can spare, plus part of the profit from every pair of shoes sold, to JC SPACE.

05Are the rocket's specifications set?

The design targets are set: nine 3 tf engines on the first stage and one 3.4 tf engine on the second, 500 kg to sun-synchronous orbit, and at least 10 flights per first stage. Calculations are yet to be confirmed by testing.

06When is the launch planned?

A target launch date has been set and will be announced. Whether it holds will depend on the results of the relevant development stages.
